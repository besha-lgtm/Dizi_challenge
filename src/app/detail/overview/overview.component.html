ABOUT THE COMPANY

VI

Visipak Industries

Corrugated Packaging Manufacturer · Est. 1998

Visipak Industries is a mid-scale corrugated packaging manufacturer based in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. Operating 6 production lines across two facilities, they serve FMCG, pharma, and e-commerce clients across South India. The company is actively investing in Industry 4.0 initiatives to reduce operational waste and improve line efficiency.

No, this challenge requires teams of 2–4 members. You can use the "Form a Team" button to find collaborators from the platform's community.

No. A video demo or high-fidelity mockup is acceptable. Physical prototypes will be considered for bonus evaluation points during the pilot phase.

Will Visipak provide facility access for testing? {{ openFaq === 2 ? '−' : '+' }}

Shortlisted teams (top 3) will be invited for a guided facility visit in Visakhapatnam. Travel allowance up to ₹3,000 per team will be reimbursed.

What happens after the pilot opportunity? {{ openFaq === 3 ? '−' : '+' }}

If selected for pilot, the winning team co-develops the solution with the Visipak engineering team over 3 months. Successful pilots may result in a paid contract or internship offer.
