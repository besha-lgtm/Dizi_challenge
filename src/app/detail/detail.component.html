Reduce Power Loss in Corrugation Plant

Visipak Industries · Visakhapatnam · Posted 12 days ago

PROBLEM STATEMENT

The industry experiences inconsistent visibility into energy consumption across production lines, resulting in increased operating cost and limited root-cause analysis. Each corrugation line operates independently with no centralized monitoring system.

CURRENT SITUATION

Energy readings are manually reviewed at the end of each shift. Machine-wise analysis is not available, and shift-level exception tracking is entirely absent. The team estimates 15–20% preventable energy waste per month.

EXPECTED OUTCOME

A low-cost monitoring model using sensors, dashboards, or machine-level data capture to identify avoidable energy loss. The solution should be deployable within 3 months with a budget under ₹50,000 and operational by non-technical staff.
